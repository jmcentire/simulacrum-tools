A generative simulacrum.

Built on the architecture from Park et al.'s Generative Agent Simulations of 1,000 People (2024) — a two-phase dispatcher routes each turn between a recall branch and an adversarial-dialog branch.

Pitch an idea, propose a frame, claim something. Professional for sharp pushback when the framing has a flaw — and direct engagement when it doesn't.
